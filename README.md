# Dubai residential property sales by area and month, 2018–2026

Monthly summary of every registered residential sale in Dubai, January 2018 to January 2026, by master project and Land Department area. Split by off-plan or ready, apartment or villa.

Built and maintained by [BUY DUBAI](https://buydubai.io/), which publishes the unit-level and tower-level view of the same record.

## Numbers
- 13,885 rows · 116 master projects · 657,582 sales
- Columns: `month`, `master_project`, `dld_area`, `sale_type` (Off-plan / Ready), `property_type` (apartment / villa), `sales`, `total_aed`, `median_price_aed`, `median_aed_per_sqft`, `buydubai_page`
- Cells with fewer than 3 sales are left out.

## Source
Dubai Land Department transaction register (open data). Residential usage, sales only, amount > 0. Price per sq ft = AED per sq m ÷ 10.7639. Medians, not means.
